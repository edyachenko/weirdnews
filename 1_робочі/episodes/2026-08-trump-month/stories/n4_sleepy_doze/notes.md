# n4_sleepy_doze — notes

## Source identification (important correction vs. the footage lead)
The footage lead in the brief guessed this was a separate "vaccine executive order /
Task Force on Vaccine Safety" signing event. That event exists and RFK Jr. does appear
in it, but it is a **different occasion**: an Independent clip (`p-3Y6_Ih5tg`, "Trump
appears sleepy during RFK's child vaccine speech") also shows Trump dozing next to RFK
Jr., but its upload date is **2026-08-11**, not August 31 — a different, earlier
dozing incident (confirmed separately by OK! Magazine's own recap, which describes
"an incident on August 11, when Trump was caught on video closing his eyes ... during
Kennedy Jr.'s speech on chronic disease initiatives" as distinct from the Aug 31 one).
**This clip was not used** in the story, specifically to avoid the dated-clip trap
called out in the contract.

The correct Aug 31, 2026 source — confirmed by upload_date `20260831`, ~57 min runtime
("hour-long event"), and OK! Magazine's narration matching almost word-for-word
(Howard Lutnick's "it's all thanks to you, because nobody thought this was possible")
— is **The White House's own YouTube upload**: "President Trump Makes an Announcement
on Healthcare Affordability, Aug. 31, 2026" (`uyeCoZ5Yego`). This was a drug-pricing
deal signing with pharma CEOs, RFK Jr., and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick. RFK
Jr. opens his remarks ~13:41 into the stream thanking Trump for his "leadership"
(roughly matches "15 minutes in" from the facts); the extended eyes-closed stretch
Daily Beast/Mediaite describe runs from roughly 20:53 to 21:29, ending right as
Lutnick's "nobody thought this was possible" line lands and Trump visibly recovers.

## Reaction/punch inserts — none found, documented per budget instructions
Per the budget cap (1 reaction-beat search query), ran:
`ytsearch15:"Trump falls asleep dozes off Oval Office RFK reaction SNL Daily Show meme"`.
Results were Jimmy Kimmel Live and Seth Meyers videos, but on checking their
transcripts neither covers this specific Aug 31 RFK/healthcare-affordability doze —
the Kimmel video references a winter cabinet-meeting nap (mentions snow/blizzard, so
a different, later incident) and the Seth Meyers "Closer Look" references a different
dozing moment (during Kennedy Center demolition talk). Checked in-frame for a free
reaction (both bystanders visible in the Independent Aug-11 clip, and the executives
standing behind Trump in the Aug-31 footage) — everyone pictured is looking straight
ahead at the speaker, composed, no visible smirk/reaction usable as an insert. Found
one candidate contrasting "Sleepy Joe" archival clip (The Telegraph, `sGZD7-El4xE`)
but its description confirms it's from a 2020 South Carolina rally — six years off,
so it was excluded rather than risk mis-dating.
**Net result: 0 reaction/punch inserts**, against the usual ≥2 default. This matches
what the budget instructions anticipated for this story ("thinner sourcing than
most... document that honestly rather than over-spending to force it").

## Visuals

stretches: RFK's opening praise ~13:33–13:52, and the doze/recovery stretch
~19:30–21:45), to stay inside budget. Visual b-roll for narr beats therefore reuses
muted versions of the four clips already cut (the one dedicated visual-only clip plus
the three SOT clips muted) — explicitly allowed per format.md ("cycle other clips
from the same story, muted SOTs too") given how thin this story's footage window is.
