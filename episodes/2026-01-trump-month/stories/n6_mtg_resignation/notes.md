# n6_mtg_resignation -- notes

## Date caveat (important)
Greene announced her resignation and Trump's "traitor"/"wacky" insults and his
"great news" reaction all happened around **November 17-24, 2025** (confirmed via
yt-dlp upload dates on the videos named in episode.json's `footage_ideas`, e.g.
News24 ab3MsZjh7hQ and BBC News zQ4dwl80glU are both `20251122`, not January).
January 5, 2026 is only the *effective date* her resignation took effect.

Per the brief's "uploads must be from Jan 2026" requirement, I did **not** use
the November clips. Instead I sourced all 5 clips from real coverage of her
actual last day in office, uploaded **January 5-6, 2026**:
- CBS Chicago and CBS Philadelphia's "last day in Congress" local-news pieces
  (both `20260105`), which recap the Nov. feud in the reporter's/anchor's own
  words -- giving real "traitor" language without a Nov.-dated clip.
- Forbes Breaking News's Jan 6 uploads of Greene's farewell-day CNN interview,
  which is where the "raped as teenagers" / "called me a traitor for standing
  with those victims" lines actually came from -- said by Greene herself, on
  her last day, not from the Nov. announcement.

Net effect: no clip is captioned with a date, and none misrepresents when the
event happened -- the narration says "on January fifth" only for the actual
effective-date beat (CBS Chicago's own line, also from Jan 5).
